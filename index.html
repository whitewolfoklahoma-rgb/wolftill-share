1. WolfTill is a record-keeping helper for your own business income, expenses, and receipt references. It is not a bank, an accountant, or tax advice. Check your numbers before you file taxes or make business decisions — you are responsible for your own books.

4. The app is provided as-is. If something doesn't work, contact us through the Help tab and we'll fix or replace the file.

Help — how WolfTill works

📸 The easy way: send Muse the receipt

Send Muse a photo of a receipt in chat and say it's for the business. Muse reads the store, date, and total, files the expense for you, and it shows up here on its own the next time you open WolfTill — no typing, no tapping an import button. The same goes for money that comes in: tell Muse who paid you and it lands in the Income list.

🏠 Home

The Home tab shows this month's money in, money out, and what's left (your net), the year so far, and your most recent entries.

💸 Adding an expense by hand

On the Expenses tab, fill in the date, who you paid, the amount, and a category, then tap Save expense. Notes and a receipt photo reference are optional but help at tax time. Tap the 🎤 mic beside a field and talk instead of typing — one tap and it's listening.

💰 Adding income by hand

On the Income tab, record who paid you, what for, and how much. Mark it Received when the money is actually in hand, Pending while you're still waiting on it. Only Received income counts in your net.

✏️ Fixing or removing an entry

Every entry has Edit and Delete buttons. Edit opens the entry in the form at the top of its tab — change anything and save. Delete asks once to be sure. If you delete an entry that arrived from Muse's inbox, it stays deleted — it won't come back.

📊 Totals & taxes

The Totals tab breaks a tax year down by month and by category — income received, expenses, and net. Use the CSV export buttons there to hand your books to a spreadsheet or your tax preparer. WolfTill keeps records; it is not tax advice.

🔒 Where your data lives

Your entries live only in this browser, on this device. WolfTill collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. Back up with the CSV exports — if you clear your browser data, entries stored only here go with it.
